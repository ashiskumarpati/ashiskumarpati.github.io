01 / PROFILE

Mathematics at the
core of intelligent systems.

I work at the intersection of mathematical theory and modern AI, with a focus on building ideas that are both rigorous and useful.

My academic background is in Mathematics, with doctoral research on Calculus of Variations with Symmetric Forms. My current work extends that foundation into Deep Learning, Computer Vision, Generative AI, Agentic AI and Quantum Machine Learning.
