About

I am an Assistant Professor at the Centre for Data Science, ITER, SOA Deemed to be University.

My academic background is in Mathematics, with an Integrated Ph.D. from IISER Kolkata. My doctoral research focused on Calculus of Variations with Symmetric Forms.

My current research interests connect mathematical thinking with modern artificial intelligence, including deep learning, computer vision, and applications of AI to problems in mathematics and science.
